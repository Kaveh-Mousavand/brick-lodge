Brick Lodge

A repository for recent developments on bricks

Introduction:

This repository is devoted to recent developments in the study of bricks in representation theory of algebras and related areas. It aims to serve as a growing collection of important results and developments from recent years, as well as a space for sharing new results, observations and examples, posing open problems and questions, and exchanging ideas. The hope is that it will provide a useful resource for researchers working on bricks and related topics, and help foster further developments and connections within the subject. Those who wish to contribute to the repository are kindly invited to get in touch with Kaveh Mousavand.
